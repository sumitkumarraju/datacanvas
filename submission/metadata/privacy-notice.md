DataCanvas processes imported CSV files and chart recipes in the add-on panel on your device. The add-on does not send CSV contents to a publisher server or use analytics, telemetry, or generative AI. DataCanvas does not require a separate publisher account.

When you insert a chart, DataCanvas sends the rendered PNG to the active Adobe Express document. The image may show values or labels from your CSV. Adobe handles the document under its own privacy terms. A downloaded PNG or recipe is saved where you choose. A recipe exported with data includes the original CSV cells. A recipe exported without data retains chart settings and requires a CSV when reopened.

If you contact the publisher for support, the publisher receives your email address and the information you choose to share. Contact sumitts317@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.
