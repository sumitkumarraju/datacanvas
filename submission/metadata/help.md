# DataCanvas Help

DataCanvas turns a UTF-8 CSV into a chart inside Adobe Express on desktop.

1. Open DataCanvas and choose **Use sample** to try it, or import your own CSV with column headers.
2. Choose one category column and one numeric column. Correct invalid numeric values in the CSV. If values are missing, choose whether to exclude the affected rows.
3. Choose a bar, line, or pie chart and check the preview. You can sort categories and keep duplicates separate, sum them, or average them.
4. Select **Add chart to Express** with an active page to insert the chart, or choose **Download PNG** to save a copy.

The add-on accepts files up to 2 MB, 5,000 data rows, and 20 columns. Pie charts require non-negative values. An inserted PNG is a flat image. Save a recipe to reuse the chart settings. A recipe exported with data includes the original CSV cells. A recipe exported without data requires you to reimport a CSV.

For help, use the support email on the DataCanvas Adobe Express listing. Include your browser, operating system, and steps to reproduce the issue. Use synthetic data when sharing an example.
