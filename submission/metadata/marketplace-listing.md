## DataCanvas

DataCanvas

Character count: 10

**Summary**

Turn CSV data into charts for your designs

Character count: 42

**Description**

DataCanvas turns local CSV data into clear bar, line, and pie charts for Adobe Express. Import a UTF-8 CSV, validate your category and numeric values, choose how duplicates and missing values are handled, and preview the exact data behind the chart. Customize the title, colors, sorting, aggregation, and number formatting, then add the result to your current Adobe Express page as a flat PNG or download it. Export a versioned recipe to preserve the mapping and style choices. Data processing happens locally in the add-on. The chart is an image, not a native editable chart; keep the recipe as the editable source.

Character count: 610

Not applicable. DataCanvas does not use generative AI or transmit CSV data to a backend.

**Support scope**

Desktop Adobe Express add-on. UTF-8 CSV input, up to 2 MB, 5,000 data rows, and 20 columns.
